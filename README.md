# BillTrackerPro — Live Demo

**[View the demo →](https://anoop6543.github.io/billtrackerpro-demo/)**

A clickable preview of BillTrackerPro, the personal finance platform. This demo runs
entirely in your browser with realistic **sample data** — no account needed, no real
bank connections, nothing leaves your device.

## What's inside

- **Dashboard** — net worth, cash flow, spending breakdown, forecasts
- **Transactions** — search, filter, and categorize
- **Budgets** — monthly limits with rollover
- **Subscriptions** — automatic recurring-charge detection
- **Investments** — holdings, tax lots, performance vs S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100

## Notes

- All data shown is fictional sample data for illustration.
- The full app connects to real bank accounts via Plaid.
